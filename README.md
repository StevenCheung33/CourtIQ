# CourtIQ

CourtIQ is a basketball court discovery platform that helps players find free outdoor courts and see useful information about them before deciding where to play.

## Problem

Finding a good basketball court can be difficult because players often do not know how crowded a court is before arriving. A court may be completely empty or have too many people to comfortably play.

Some basketball courts, such as courts at elementary, middle, and high schools, may also be available for public use but can be difficult to discover because they are not clearly listed as basketball courts on services like Google Maps.

## Target Users

CourtIQ is designed primarily for casual basketball players looking for free outdoor courts. Players could use the platform whether they want to practice alone, play with friends, or find other people to play basketball with.

## Core Features

- Find basketball courts within a selected radius
- View the estimated number of people currently at a court
- View court conditions and information
- Read and submit court reviews
- Filter courts based on player preferences

## Court Data

Each court may include information such as:

- Number of basketball hoops
- Photos of the court
- Court condition
- Lighting availability
- Location
- Estimated number of players currently at the court
- User reviews

## What Makes CourtIQ Different

CourtIQ focuses specifically on information basketball players care about when deciding where to play rather than only showing the location of a court.

One of its main features will be showing an estimate of how many people are currently at a court, helping players avoid traveling to a court only to find that it is empty or overcrowded.
